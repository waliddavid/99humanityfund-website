# Part 3: Manufacturing the World Fairly

<div class="part-hero">
  <img src="/cards/part-3.jpg" alt="Manufacturing redistributing across the globe from a few dense clusters toward the regions that need the jobs most — Part 3." />
</div>

## A dividend keeps people alive. Meaningful work — that pays well, has dignity, and exists where people actually live — is what makes a life worth living.

Part 1 proposed the fund. Part 2 built the institution. Part 3 asks where its factories should be. Today manufacturing is concentrated in a handful of countries — 62% of it in just five — leaving billions of working-age adults far from any factory that could hire them. This part presents a 25-year model across 194 countries and 30 manufacturing categories: five scenarios, one moderate proposal, and an honest accounting of what redistributing 41 million jobs can and cannot fix. The full essay, the complete methodology, and the underlying data are all here to read and to download.
