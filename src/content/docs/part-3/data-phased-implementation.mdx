# Data: Phased Implementation

<div class="article-hero">
  <img src="/cards/part-3-4.jpg" alt="A landscape built up in stages — the five-tranche rollout from 2027 to 2056." />
</div>

<p class="dl-row"><a class="dl-button" href="/downloads/phased-implementation.csv" download>↓ Download the full CSV (194 countries, 30 columns)</a></p>

This is the year-by-year rollout file behind the Part 3 timeline. It traces each country's manufacturing employment across the five 5-year tranches — 2027, 2031, 2036, 2041, 2046, 2051, and 2056 — under two scenarios: **Informal Insertion** (the proposed implementation) and **Balanced** (what fuller fairness would require). For each scenario it also gives the per-tranche change (T1 through T5) and the total 2027→2056 change.

All employment figures are in **thousands of jobs**. So China's 2027 figure of roughly 215,000 means about 215 million workers. The planning phase runs 2027–2031 (no employment changes), then Tranche 1 begins; that's why the 2027 and 2031 columns are identical for every country.

## Explore the data

Toggle between the Informal Insertion and Balanced scenarios, sort any column by clicking its header, search by country, and filter by region or income group. Click anywhere on the table — or the button below — to open it full screen.

## What the columns mean

The downloadable CSV has 30 columns: identity fields plus two parallel scenario blocks (Informal Insertion and Balanced), each with seven milestone-year values, five per-tranche deltas, and a total delta.

Data sources include UN WPP 2024, ILO ILOSTAT, UNIDO INDSTAT, UN Comtrade, World Bank WGI/WDI/LPI, UNESCO UIS, USGS, and the Energy Institute — all consulted May 2026.</p>
