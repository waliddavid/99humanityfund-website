## If the case for the fund is this strong, what is the honest case against it?

A proposal that cannot face its own hardest questions has not earned belief. This final part is the reckoning: how the fund avoids becoming what it replaces, the ways it could genuinely fail, how movements like it get killed, the strongest criticisms stated fairly — and the decision that, in the end, belongs to everyone.
